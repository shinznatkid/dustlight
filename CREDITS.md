people and keeps its own license. None of it is stored in this repository:

release build ships it next to the game. The in-game Credits screen lists the same.

## Inspiration

The game's loop (clean up, repair and decorate abandoned rooms, no timers or scores) is
inspired by *Hozy* (Come On Studio / tinyBuild). No names, text, art or music from it are
used.
